Negotiation Pro

An interactive negotiation learning platform for sales professionals using Articulate and GPTS

Project Overview

This is a course project for Tools for Online Learning. I leveraged the benefits of different EdTech tools to solve a problem in professional training.

Problem

Sales professionals often need to negotiate with their clients. However, they face the problem of a lack of education or training in negotiation. The traditional training methods are often time-consuming, lack personalized feedback, and fail to provide real-time, scenario-based learning experiences. 

This results in a gap in skills application under dynamic market conditions and customer expectations, directly impacting sales success and long-term business relationships. 

Solution

We designed a tool for learning and practicing negotiation skills to reach win-win solutions using psychological factors of influence.

Learning Science Principles

After understanding our learners, sales professionals, we chose six learning science principles that worked for their contexts to guide our design.

Implementation

Ideally, we aimed to build a platform that would incorporate onboarding, negotiation course content, and AI negotiation scenarios so that learners can learn and practice. However, due to time and technical constraints, we built our prototypes on several platforms, including Figma (UI design), Articulate Rise (course content), and Chat GPTS (negotiation practice).

Onboarding

To increase learners' motivation, the course uses a gamification element: learners will earn coins to unlock new courses if they reach a negotiation.

The channeling principle is also employed to limit learners' choices: learners cannot choose the next course unless they finish the current course.

Articulate Course Demo

The negotiation course uses content from Cialdini's Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion, introducing 6 principles of persuasion. The courses are designed to be micro lessons: sales professionals are busy with their work, so the learning should be effective and short.

Negotiation Process

The negotiation process provides a conversational experience for learners. I drafted the prompt to detect if learners use the appropriate principle. Learners will have 3 attempts to apply the principle - otherwise the negotiation will end.
